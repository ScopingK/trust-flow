How TrustFlow Works

TrustFlow is an adaptive fraud prevention layer designed for digital banking customers.

Instead of abruptly blocking transfers or bombarding users with technical alerts, it looks at:

  • Recipient reliability and name match consistency
  • Time of transaction and unusual device parameters
  • Whether high-pressure scam language is driving the payment

When risks cross critical levels, a designated family contact is brought in as a calm second check.
